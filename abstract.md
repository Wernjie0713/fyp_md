# Abstract

This project developed a company-owned reporting continuity and report reconstruction platform for Marrybrown to reduce operational dependence on a third-party cloud point-of-sale (POS) reporting portal in a large-scale food and beverage (F&B) enterprise. In the previous operating setting, Finance and Operations stakeholders relied on vendor-managed exports and portal interfaces for routine reconciliation and month-end closing, which constrained ad hoc analysis, limited transparency into underlying transactions, and could delay issue resolution when external reporting services were degraded or unavailable. The implemented platform replicated selected source-system tables into a Microsoft SQL Server reporting warehouse through controlled 1:1 replication, preserving raw data fidelity and traceability while supporting safe refresh and rerun behaviour within defined operational boundaries. Report calculations were reconstructed at a semantic layer exposed through a FastAPI service, enabling schema-on-read transformations that kept business rules versioned and auditable while decoupling report delivery from direct database implementation details. An internal React-based web portal was delivered to provide validated report search, viewing, export, and administration functions in a real company deployment environment. The implementation followed an iterative, validation-driven approach in which report requirements were reverse engineered from vendor-facing report behaviour and stakeholder needs, then verified through reconciliation-driven parity validation against vendor portal outputs for matched outlets, date ranges, and filter scopes. The project successfully delivered the retained active reporting platform, including data replication, backend report reconstruction, portal access, validation workflows, and deployment support. However, Product Mix Report, Discount Remark Report, and Product Mix with modifier without ETL were not fully closed because reverse engineering and parity validation against the vendor portal were not sufficient to isolate the remaining profit logic required for final closure. Overall, the project delivered a validated internal reporting platform that improved reporting continuity, strengthened company control over report logic and access, and provided a maintainable foundation for ongoing operational reporting.
